The European SACT Regimen Catalog Implementation Guide is developed and published by the PHOENIX initiative.

### Major Contributors and Their Roles

- **PHOENIX** publishes the guide and coordinates its development.
- **Oncology centres, clinical oncologists and oncology pharmacists** provide the clinical knowledge required to represent regimens in a clinically meaningful and safe way.
- **Catalog publishers** (national and regional cancer agencies, hospital networks, professional societies) contribute requirements and existing catalog content.
- **Vendors and implementers** of oncology electronic prescribing systems contribute implementation experience and feedback.
- **The FHIR and oncology informatics community** contributes modelling experience, review and editorial work.
