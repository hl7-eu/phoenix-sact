### Standards and specifications

- [HL7 FHIR R4](http://hl7.org/fhir/R4/), in particular the [PlanDefinition](http://hl7.org/fhir/R4/plandefinition.html), [ActivityDefinition](http://hl7.org/fhir/R4/activitydefinition.html) and [Library](http://hl7.org/fhir/R4/library.html) resources.
- [HL7 Europe Common Cancer Model](https://build.fhir.org/ig/hl7-eu/cancer-common): European common data model for cancer.
- [HL7 FHIR Clinical Practice Guidelines (CPG-on-FHIR)](http://hl7.org/fhir/uv/cpg/): patterns for computable guidelines and order sets.
- [HL7 US mCODE](http://hl7.org/fhir/us/mcode/): minimal Common Oncology Data Elements.

### Terminologies

- [WHO ATC classification](https://atcddd.fhi.no/atc_ddd_index/): anticancer and supportive agents (substance level).
- [SNOMED CT](https://www.snomed.org/): clinical findings, intents, substances.
- [EDQM Standard Terms](https://standardterms.edqm.eu/): routes of administration.
- [UCUM](https://ucum.org/): dose units and dose basis.
- [LOINC](https://loinc.org/): laboratory tests used for monitoring.
- [ICD-10](https://icd.who.int/browse10/): cancer conditions.
- NCI Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE): toxicity grading used in dose modification rules.

### Clinical guidelines

- ESMO Clinical Practice Guidelines.
- MASCC/ESMO antiemetic guidelines (emetogenic risk classification).
