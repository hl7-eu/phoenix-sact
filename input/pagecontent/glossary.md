### Glossary

| Term | Definition | Also known as |
|---|---|---|
| **SACT** | Systemic Anti-Cancer Therapy: medicinal treatment of cancer acting on the whole body (chemotherapy, targeted therapy, immunotherapy, endocrine therapy, antibody-drug conjugates). | Systemic therapy, antineoplastic therapy |
| **Regimen** | Standardised definition of a SACT treatment: combination of agents, doses, routes and schedule, organised in cycles, for one or more indications. It is a definition, not the treatment of a specific patient. | Protocol, chemotherapy protocol, treatment schedule |
| **Regimen catalog** | Curated, versioned and governed collection of regimen definitions published by an authority. | Regimen library, protocol library |
| **Indication** | Clinical context for which a regimen is intended: cancer condition, histology, stage, biomarkers, intent, setting, line of therapy. | |
| **Intent** | Goal of the treatment: curative or palliative. | Treatment intent |
| **Setting** | Strategic timing of the regimen with respect to other treatment modalities: neoadjuvant, adjuvant, concomitant (with radiotherapy). | |
| **Line of therapy** | Ordinal position of the regimen in the sequence of treatments for advanced or metastatic disease. | Treatment line |
| **Cycle** | Period of time over which the components of a regimen are administered, after which the pattern repeats (e.g. 21 days). | Course |
| **Component** | A medicinal agent administered as part of a regimen cycle, either as anticancer agent or as supportive medication. | Regimen element, drug line |
| **Agent** | Active substance administered as a component. | Drug, substance |
| **Dose basis** | Parameter used to calculate the individual dose: body surface area (mg/m2), body weight (mg/kg), target AUC (mg/mL.min, Calvert formula) or flat dose (mg). | Dose calculation method |
| **Supportive medication** | Medication administered to prevent or treat adverse effects of anticancer agents: premedication, antiemetics, hydration, growth factors, rescue agents. | Supportive care |
| **Emetogenic risk** | Risk of nausea and vomiting caused by a regimen, categorised as high, moderate, low or minimal (MASCC/ESMO). | Emetogenicity |
| **Monitoring requirement** | Test or assessment to be performed before or during treatment, with the threshold required to proceed. | Pre-treatment checks |
| **Dose modification rule** | Rule describing how doses are reduced, delayed, omitted or discontinued in case of toxicity or organ dysfunction. | Dose adjustment |
